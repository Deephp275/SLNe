About Us

Shree Lakshmi Narayan Enterprise

Shree Lakshmi Narayan Enterprise supplies plywood, laminates and timber for furniture, interiors and construction projects.

Our range includes plywood from Greenply, Kitply, Cubixa and Jansathi in different thicknesses; plain colour, designer and 3D print laminates; and silver timber in ready cut sizes.

We help you choose the right material for your project, and welcome both individual and bulk enquiries.
